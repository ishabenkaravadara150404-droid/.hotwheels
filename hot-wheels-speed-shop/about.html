ABOUT SPEED SHOP

Fueled by passion for die-cast miniature engineering and high-speed gravity track racing.

Origin & Purpose

BRINGING RACING DREAMS TO LIFE

Hot Wheels – Speed Shop was created with a clear purpose: to build an interactive, exciting, and accessible online store for die-cast model collectors and toy car enthusiasts across India.

Since 1968, Hot Wheels has captured the imagination of millions with its signature 1:64 scale metal cars, roaring hot rods, and gravity-defying orange tracks. What starts as a small toy car often grows into a lifelong passion for automotive design, speed, and track building. Speed Shop celebrates that passion with a curated selection of iconic models.

What Makes Speed Shop Special

Authentic Die-Cast

Every toy car featured in our store uses genuine die-cast metal bodies with fine detailing, durable paint, and smooth wheels.

Protective Packing

Die-cast collectors love mint condition packaging. We ensure all cars are packaged safely to prevent bends and scratches.

Fast Dispatch

Orders are prepared quickly so young racers and adult collectors can put their new cars onto the track as soon as possible.

About This College Project

This website was developed by a first-year Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) student. The project focuses on creating a clean, structured front-end application using semantic HTML5, custom CSS3 styling, responsive Bootstrap 5 layout grids, and clean Vanilla JavaScript for client-side functionality.
